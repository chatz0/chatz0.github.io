Research

My work sits where distributed systems, machine learning and applied cryptography meet. I study how to split computation and trust across many parties, and how to give each of them a reason to take part honestly.

Distributed & split learning

Training models across resource-constrained clients and edge helpers: scheduling and workflow optimisation for parallel and multi-hop split learning, and decentralised federated learning.

Distributed ledgers & auditing

Ledgers that run on mobile devices, and cryptographic protocols that keep transactions consistent and private across independent organisations, for example in financial auditing.

Incentives & reputation

Mechanism design for device-to-device ecosystems and crowdsourcing: rewarding contributors, building reputation without a central authority, and verifying work anonymously.

Mobile & edge computing

Offloading computation between phones, cloudlets and the cloud, and the systems behind mobile augmented reality.
